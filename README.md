For the .svg file, Drag the svg into an about:blank, and then click "Choose File". Choose your .html, then click "Open HTML".

For the .xhtml, just download it, open the .xhtml, click "Choose File", then choose the HTML file you want, and then click "Open in about:blank". You could also just drag and drop it into an about:blank too.

For the .xml, it is the same thing as the .svg. Just drag the .xml into an about:blank, click "Choose File", choose your HTML file, then it will automatically open it into an about:blank.
